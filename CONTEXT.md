# Domain language

## Canonical Catalog

The document containing Complete Variants and catalog-wide definitions for Smart, Fast, and Cheap.

## Model Variant

One DeepSWE configuration identified by model checkpoint, reasoning effort, and evaluation harness.

## Smart

DeepSWE pass@1. Higher is better.

## Fast

Mean end-to-end DeepSWE trial duration in seconds per task. Lower is better.

## Cheap

Mean reported inference cost in USD per DeepSWE task. Lower is better.

## Complete Variant

A Model Variant with finite Smart, finite positive Fast, and finite positive Cheap values. Only Complete Variants enter the Canonical Catalog.

## Source Artifact

An official DeepSWE release artifact identified by URL and SHA-256 checksum.
